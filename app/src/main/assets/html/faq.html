What is this app for?
This app is designed to track your smartphone usage as part of a research study on digital wellness. It provides you with insights into your phone usage patterns and allows you to set usage limits for specific apps.

What is the study about?
The study aims to understand smartphone usage patterns among students in Chicago. We're investigating how individuals aged 18-24 interact with and feel about their phones.

What will I get out of this study?
By participating, you'll gain deeper insights into your smartphone usage habits. You'll be able to see your daily and weekly screen time for individual apps, set usage limits, and potentially optimize your digital habits. Your participation will also contribute to important research on digital wellness. Other incentives include - gift cards and a certificate of participation signed by the study's principal investigators.

Why is my usage being shown incorrectly?
Sometimes, the app might display inflated screen-time usage values. This is because on some phones, the 'multi-window' feature - a feature that allows you to have multiple apps open in the foreground at the same time interferes with the app’s ability to capture data. While your total time spent value might look inflated, we are able to rectify this in the backend and see your data with correct usage values.

What exactly is the app recording?
The app tracks screen time usage and notification data.

Who has access to my data?
Your data is accessible only to the research team. All personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and your data is stored securely.

How is my data used in the study?
Your data is analyzed in aggregated and anonymized form. Individual-level data is never shared or published. We use the collective data to understand overall usage patterns.

Can I delete my data?
Yes, you can request to have your data deleted at any time by contacting our research team. This will not affect your participation in other aspects of the study.

How secure is my data?
We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All transmitted and stored data is encrypted.

What happens to my data after the study ends?
After the study concludes, personally identifiable information will be permanently deleted. Anonymized data may be retained for further analysis or archived according to research data retention policies.
